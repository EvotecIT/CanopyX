Interactive data views

CanopyX examples

Explore large trees, inspect records, and try host-governed actions. These generated examples use synthetic data and work straight from disk.

  • Calendar · Quick start

    Team calendar

    A calendar from a list of events and nothing else: month, week, day and agenda, read-only, with a details popover. One option at a time adds the simple layouts, a scheduler with creating, dropping work from a grid, sources and context menus, right to left and a timeline without resources. Also in a dark palette.

  • Calendar

    Operations calendar

    Month, week, day, three days, agenda and year views of maintenance windows, changes, incidents and expiries, with repeating windows, a change freeze, "+N more", drag to reschedule and the record panel. Also in a dark palette, in Polish and German.

  • Calendar · Resource timeline

    Incidents by host

    Hosts grouped by site and role, zoom from hours to quarters, capacity per group, busy hours and overlaps per host, and drag across hosts with overlaps refused. Also in a dark palette; resource columns show engineers in the day view. The 48-room schedule adds a shared recurring event, an occurrence editor and an optional JSON feed.

  • Calendar · Facets

    Certificate expiries

    Three thousand certificates with a facet panel, a busy month and a timeline per issuer; password expiries as an all-day agenda per person. Also in a dark palette.

  • Calendar · Linked views

    Calendar and grid

    Select a day or slots to filter the grid; select rows to highlight their events. Also in a dark palette.

  • Flow · Guided setup

    Connect a mail server

    A guided flow with choice cards, a password that never echoes, certificate options that appear only for secure connections, a recipients list and the review page with change links. Also schedule a report on the vertical stepper, a dialog-sized task (in Polish and right to left), editing settings with a changes-only review, and a dark palette.

  • Languages

    Translated reports

    Complete labels in Polish, German and Spanish, with paging, selection and filters. Each page also has a dark palette.

  • Content sizing

    Content-sized columns

    Start with widths measured from loaded records, adjust them in Columns and save a view. Also available in a dark palette.

  • Measured rows

    Wrapped observations

  • Column layout

    Sizing, priority and pinning

    Content-sized columns in wide, narrow and phone containers, optional columns that hide before scrolling, and columns pinned left and right. Also available in a dark palette.
