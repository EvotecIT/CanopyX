Live data

Rows change in place while you read: your sort, filters, search, selection, focus, scroll position and an open record stay where they are.

Endpoint monitor

A stream updates a few endpoints several times a second; the grid applies the changes together every quarter of a second and flashes the cells that changed. Rows keep their places when their latency changes, and the prompt offers to re-sort.

Job queue from a server

The grid asks its source every four seconds whether the data changed. An unchanged revision changes nothing; a new one updates the rows it shows. Polling pauses while the page is hidden.
