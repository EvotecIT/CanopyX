Column sizing, priority and pinning

Columns take the width of their header and content. Spare width goes to flex columns, then the identity column, then the widest text column. When space runs short, normal columns shrink to their minimum, optional columns hide, and only then the grid scrolls with its pinned columns in place. Drag the lower-right corner of a frame to resize it.
