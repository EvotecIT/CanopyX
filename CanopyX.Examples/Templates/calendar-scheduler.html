Team scheduler

Click or drag over empty time to add an event, drag one to move it, and drag a task from the list below onto the calendar to schedule it (or press F2 on a task, then Enter on a slot). Switch sources in the legend. Right-click, long-press or press Shift+F10 for an event's or a slot's actions. Time outside office hours is shaded.
